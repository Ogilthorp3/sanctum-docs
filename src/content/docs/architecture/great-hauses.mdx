## 1. Architectural Foundation: Headless Core & Presentation Split

Determinism comes first. To guarantee cross-platform portability between Metal 4.0 desktop builds and visionOS spatial immersion, Great Hauses enforces a strict separation between chess logic and 3D presentation:

| Layer | Primary Modules | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| **3D Presentation Layer** | `BoardView`, `PieceView`, `DuelDirector`, `DragonSpectator` | Camera choreography, spatial lighting, banner multi-meshes, audio stingers |
| **Cinematics & VFX Engine** | `MomentGovernor`, `MomentScore`, `MomentContext`, `CaptureLedger` | Situational scoring, time-scale slow-mo curves, camera swoops |
| **Gamification Engine** | `HoloChessGamification` | Dejarik projector pylons, living breathing idles, floating combat badges, victory flourishes |
| **Coach & Training Engine** | `CoachEngine`, `CoachOverlay` | Real-time threat radar, blunder detection, opening master book, tactical move explanations |
| **Deterministic Engine** | `ChessState`, `ChessMove`, `ChessAI` | Pure `RefCounted` FEN parsing, pseudo-legal moves, minimax evaluation |

The deterministic core operates completely free of scene tree dependencies. The entire chess rule engine, AI difficulty curves, and cinematic timing gates run headlessly in continuous integration without allocating GPU contexts. It runs fast. It runs everywhere.

## 2. Military-Grade 3-Tier VFX Moments System

Kill animations in tactical games usually fail in one of two directions. Either every capture triggers an exhausting five-second cutscene, or nothing happens and pieces quietly slide off the board like disappointed ghosts. We wanted drama. We did not want boredom.

We designed a situational scoring and reservoir governor architecture to strike the exact middle:

### The Scoring Hierarchy

Every piece capture is evaluated in real-time across multiple tactical dimensions:
- **Material Delta:** Queen sacrifices, piece exchanges, and pawn promotions.
- **King Proximity & Pressure:** Attacks launched directly inside the opponent's castled perimeter.
- **Blunder & Turning-Point Swings:** Evaluation swings probed via background depth probes.
- **Grudge & Revenge Multipliers:** Tracking historical piece rivalries across the match.

| Tier | Name | Target Cadence | Presentation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 2** | Showstopper | 5-8% of captures | Deep time dilation (`Engine.time_scale` to 0.15), dynamic low-angle camera swoop, audio stinger |
| **Tier 1** | Flourish | 15-20% of captures | Tactical slow-mo (0.32), close-up profile cut, spark bursts |
| **Tier 0** | Standard | 75-80% of captures | Clean, fast strikes with zero gameplay delay |

### The Reservoir Governor

The `MomentGovernor` regulates cinematic cadence. It prevents visual fatigue by tracking recent capture density. Showstopper slow-mo curves are reserved for genuine match-defining moments.

```gdscript
# Moment scoring evaluation
var score := MomentScore.evaluate(context, ledger)
var tier := governor.allocate_tier(score)
director.play_duel(attacker, victim, {"tier": tier}, strike_callback)
```

<Aside type="tip">
The governor uses a reservoir token model. If two tactical fireworks happen on consecutive moves — such as a Queen sacrifice followed immediately by a retaliatory back-rank mate — the governor grants the second event a showstopper pass regardless of cooldown.
</Aside>

## 3. The Carmack Optimization: 60/120 FPS Skeletal Animation

During early testing of the dragon's ashfall sweep, skeletal animation stuttered. Bone playback chopped whenever the camera tracked the playhead.

### The Bottleneck

Godot's `AnimationPlayer.seek(t, true)` forced synchronous skeletal re-evaluation on every frame. Large skinned meshes with dozens of bones flushed the vertex cache repeatedly. The CPU-GPU pipeline choked.

### The Solution

We applied the principle documented in [The Carmack Optimization](/architecture/carmack-optimization/): understand what the silicon actually wants.

```gdscript
# Carmack Optimization in dragon_rig.gd
anim.seek(maxf(t, 0.0), false) # update: false lets Godot's bone interpolation cache run smoothly
```

Switching to asynchronous bone interpolation eliminated pipeline stalls. The frame rate locked. Desktop Metal runs at solid **60 FPS**; Apple Vision Pro glides at **120 FPS**.

<Aside type="note">
Never force the renderer to flush transform caches when interpolation can compute the delta asynchronously. The hardware knows how to blend. Let it blend.
</Aside>

## 4. Zelda-Grade Aesthetics & Terminator 2 Charred Remains

When the dragon incinerates the fallen army, we refused to let the pieces simply fade away. Drawing inspiration from *The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild* and *Terminator 2*, the battlefield keeps its scars:

1. **Weathered Calcified Bone PBR Materials:**
   - **Albedo:** Natural aged ivory with charcoal soot gradients (`Color(0.22, 0.20, 0.19)`).
   - **Roughness:** Chalky `0.92` matte calcified surface.
   - **Internal Warmth:** Faint, grounded amber ember traces in bone fissures (`Color(0.85, 0.35, 0.08) * 0.04`), avoiding cheap neon glows.
2. **Persistent Battlefield Monuments:**
   - Rather than sinking through the floor and despawning, the defeated army's charred skeletons remain collapsed on their tiles throughout the endgame. The board becomes a grim and majestic graveyard.

Chess is ancient, but it never has to be static. Inspired by the animated holographic HoloChess game aboard the *Millennium Falcon*, we brought the board to life:

- **Check Alert Choreography:** When the King is placed in check, the sovereign shudders while defending knights and footmen brace forward behind raised shields.
- **Victory Flourishes:** Upon landing a kill, the capturing piece performs a rank-specific celebration. Pawns hop and brandish swords. Knights rear their mounts on hind legs. Bishops levitate with mystic pulses. Queens execute a graceful combat pirouette.
- **Arcade Momentum & Floating Badges:** Dynamic holographic badges rise above the tiles on critical strikes, royal forks, and multi-kill combo streaks.

## 7. The Grandmaster Fast Improvement Coach Engine

To help players rapidly improve their tactical vision and prepare for rematches against challenging rivals, Great Hauses embeds the **Coach Engine**:

- **Real-Time Threat Radar:** Evaluates all squares every ply. Hanging undefended pieces are flagged immediately with red alerts and coordinates.
- **Strategic Move Explanation:** Recommends the highest-yield master move with plain-language explanations of tactical purpose (central control, pins, forks, King safety).
- **Opening Master Book:** Recognizes common openings (King's Pawn, Queen's Gambit, London System, Sicilian) and guides optimal piece development.
- **Interactive Training Shortcuts:** Pressing `C` toggles the coach overlay panel; pressing `T` illuminates the best tactical move and target tile on the 3D board.
