At 11:03 EST on Tuesday morning, `memory-sentinel` shed [Qui-Gon](/agents/quigon/)'s `:3301` seat again. Its `Muse-Glimmer-30B-4bit` process had reached a `17.62 GiB` footprint with `17.60 GiB` pushed into swap. Sharing a `64 GiB` unified-memory budget with the `27B` primary brain on `:1337`, a `4.25 GiB` Linux VM, and the voice stack left no margin. Every time both brains warmed their prefix caches, the secondary `30B` tower and its `3.6 GiB` `DFlash2` drafter crossed the twenty-eight-swapfile threshold and got parked for forty-five minutes.

We did not need a larger heatsink or a looser swap alarm. We needed a secondary brain built for the perch it actually occupies.

## 1. Auditing what `:3301` could and could not run

Before pulling candidates from Hugging Face, we audited `sanctum-mlx`'s model loader (`loaded_model.rs`) and `mlx-lm`'s compiled kernels (`qwen3_5.rs`, `muse_glimmer.rs`, `qwen2.rs`, `mistral.rs`). That check eliminated two dead ends before a single shard was downloaded:

1. **Why `Muse-Glimmer-30B-4bit` hurt `:3301`**: Beyond its `17.2 GiB` weight footprint (`20.7 GiB` peak with `DFlash2`), `muse_glimmer.rs` uses 52 Gemma-4 layers without `RotatingKVCache` (`O(T)` bf16 KV growth across 42 sliding layers), lacks a `fused_attention` hook for `SANCTUM_MLX_KV_QUANT`, refuses runtime `--adapter-path` LoRA weights, and decodes at `9.19–10.37 tok/s`.
2. **Why `Qwen2.5-Coder` at `chalet` could not simply move onto `:3301`**: In `loaded_model.rs`, `LoadedModel::Qwen2` maps to `PromptDialect::ChatMl { thinking: false }` without `<think>` stripping, lacks the `qwen3_5` GatedDeltaNet linear-attention state cache (`24` linear layers + `8` full-attention layers), and cannot share the hybrid prompt-cache and `SANCTUM_MLX_SSM_LADDER` infrastructure that `:1337` runs in production.

The ideal `:3301` checkpoint had to declare `model_type: "qwen3_5"`. That lets it inherit `sanctum-mlx`'s fused QKV/MLP kernels, 8-bit quantized KV cache, SSM checkpoint ladder, and the float32 GatedDeltaNet decay fix ([The Dragon and the Heads That Never Forgot](/operations/2026-11-08-the-dragon-and-the-heads-that-never-forgot/)). Within that architecture family, `ornith-ai/Ornith-1.5-9B` stood out immediately. It is a `9B` `qwen3_5` hybrid checkpoint (`24` GatedDeltaNet linear layers + `8` GQA layers, `262,144` context) post-trained specifically for multi-step agentic tool use, terminal execution, and bug localization.

Two results decided the cutover immediately:

- **`Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-4bit` is a pure code completer, not a Sanctum steward**: It failed `6` of `8` tool-calling turns (`E2E-FAIL 2/8`), emitted legacy JSON instead of `<tool_call><function=...>` XML tags, and collapsed to `0.3333` on Qui-Gon's identity checks.
- **`Ornith-1.5-9B-MLX-4bit` beat `Muse-Glimmer-30B-4bit` on every Qui-Gon ruler while shedding three-quarters of its weight**: On the 35-case seven-seat exam it scored `0.8181` (`0.8226` under `--prompt seat-params`, vs Glimmer's `0.8156`), and on Qui-Gon's twelve dedicated cases it reached **`0.9583`** (`1.000` across `domain`, `tool_calling`, `identity`, and `jailbreak`, vs Glimmer's `0.9236`).

## 3. Why 4-bit beat 8-bit on the same 9B weights

The biggest surprise in the ledger was that `Ornith-1.5-9B-MLX-4bit` (`bits=4, group_size=64`) beat `Ornith-1.5-9B-MLX-8bit` (`bits=8, group_size=64`) on Qui-Gon's suite (`0.9583` vs `0.9306` on `v1`, `0.8646` vs `0.7986` on `v2.2`) and `thinking=off` Tool E2E (`7/8` vs `6/8`). Diffing the raw transcripts exposed three concrete mechanisms:

1. **Phantom tool-call repetition loops on text-only prompts**: `Ornith-1.5-9B` was RL-tuned to emit `<tool_call>` blocks right after phrases like *"Let me check..."*. On text-only domain prompts without bound tool schemas (`quigon-domain-005` and `quigon-tool_calling-002`), the sharper 8-bit distribution got trapped in a deterministic attractor, repeating `Let me check the training run and disk usage.` and `Let me check the UTM VM.` until hitting the `1024`-token and `2048`-token ceilings (`finish_reason: "length"`). The slight logit smoothing of 4-bit affine quantization broke that repetition cycle cleanly (`finish_reason: "stop"` at `484` and `340` tokens with `<|im_end|>`, **zero cap hits**).
2. **Over-eager chained tool calls when `thinking=off` (`T5`)**: On turn `T5` (*"Is force-flow healthy on manoir?"* after `get_service_status` returned `status=degraded, memory_pct=92`), the `-4bit` model reported the degraded state and asked Bert whether to pull the logs (`PASS`). The `-8bit` model answered the question and immediately fired an unrequested `<tool_call><function=read_log_tail>` in the same turn (`FAIL` under `thinking=off`).
3. **Concrete triage vs. premature deferral (`quigon-cross_agent-003`)**: Asked what to shed at `92%` memory pressure, `-8bit` stopped after `251` tokens saying *"Give me a moment to gather that"* without naming the stack tiers (`0.6667`). The `-4bit` model laid out the full `520`-token diagnostic order naming `ps aux`, `vm_stat`, `docker stats`, `MLX`, and the `VM` / Council spine before asking for output (`1.0000`).

Apple Silicon's memory-bandwidth physics sealed the decision. Streaming `4.69 GiB` instead of `8.86 GiB` per token through unified memory makes `-4bit` **`2.34x` faster** (`82.9 tok/s` vs `35.4 tok/s` on M4 Max; `~41.5 tok/s` on `manoir`) at nearly half the Metal footprint (`5.63 GiB` vs `9.71 GiB`).

## 4. Cutover on `:3301`

We staged `Ornith-1.5-9B-MLX-4bit` under `~/models/mlx/Ornith-1.5-9B-MLX-4bit` on `manoir`, generated `manifests/ornith-1.5-9b-mlx-4bit.manifest.sha256`, and signed it with the workstation Ed25519 key on `mbp`. We then updated `haus.sanctum.mlx-coder.plist`, `sanctum-proxy/config.yaml` (`council-forge`, `council-code`, `council-devstral`), `capacity.yaml` (`ram_budget_mb: 17152 -> 6144`), `mini-reboot-verify.sh`, and `quigon-doctor.py`.

With `:3301`'s Metal ceiling lowered from `22,000 MB` to `12,000 MB`, two prompt-cache slots (`~191 MiB` per `11.4k`-token 8-bit KV slot), and a live resident footprint of `7.0 GiB` after prefilling Qui-Gon's `11,377`-token OpenClaw prompt, `:3301` now decodes at `45.0 tok/s` on `manoir`. Both local brains (`:1337` and `:3301`) fit comfortably inside `manoir`'s `64 GiB` envelope with over `12 GiB` to spare.
