At 20:00 EDT on September 12, 2026, [Jocasta](/operations/2026-08-25-the-haus-that-healed-its-senses/) delivered her scheduled evening intelligence briefing over Signal. Right alongside actual household calendar events—a cycliste grand prix and an appointment—sat three unexpected entries:

```
📅 Upcoming:
• tomorrow — Human Being Max Kit's Birthday (age 43)
• tomorrow — Mario Limperis's Birthday (age 49)
• tomorrow — Max Kit's Birthday (age 43)
• tomorrow at 9:00 AM - 10:00 AM — Grand prix cycliste
• tomorrow at 12:20 PM - 1:20 PM — RDV Harina
```

The briefing had succumbed to noise. Two problems were immediately apparent:
1. **Casual acquaintance flood**: Bert did not need alerts for Max Kit or Mario Limperis, whose contacts had been swept from historic address book imports.
2. **Duplicate pollution**: Max Kit appeared twice—once under `Human Being Max Kit` (a corporate-prefixed contact shell) and once as `Max Kit`. Because Jocasta limits upcoming items to eight entries, noisy birthdays were crowding out legitimate commitments.

## The Raw Calendar Leak

Tracing the pipeline in [jocasta-preflight.py](/operations/2026-08-20-three-nodes-one-inbox/) revealed the source: `icalBuddy` was invoked with no calendar exclusions:

```python
cmd = ["icalBuddy", "-n", "-nc", "-b", "", "-iep", "title,datetime,location", "-eep", "notes,attendees,url", rng]
```

On macOS, the built-in Calendar subsystem synthesizes events for all contacts in AddressBook under a synthetic `Birthdays` calendar. Furthermore, synced Exchange accounts maintain an additional `Birthdays1` calendar. Querying without exclusion pulled every contact across more than 4,000 address book records, generating duplicate entries whenever a contact existed across both stores.

## Making Sanctum CRM Pristine

The solution required cleaning the source of truth in [Sanctum CRM](/operations/2026-08-11-the-archive-that-said-nothing/) rather than patching superficial regexes.

### 1. Contact Deduplication (`crm-make-pristine.py`)
While deal deduplication had previously landed in `crm-make-pristine.py`, contact records remained un-deduplicated across five disparate ingest streams (Affinity, Apple Contacts, iMessage, WhatsApp, Signal). 

We extended `crm-make-pristine.py`:
- **Name Normalization**: Strips honorifics and organizational artifacts (e.g. `Human Being Max Kit` normalizes to `max kit`).
- **Deterministic Keeper Ranking**: Priority ranks entries by Affinity deal identity, presence of birthday, presence of email, and overall populated field count.
- **Field Merging**: Any non-empty attribute on duplicate records missing on the keeper (such as phone numbers, emails, or birthdays) is copied to the keeper before archiving.
- **Reversible Archival**: Duplicates are marked `archived_reason = 'dup-contact'` rather than deleted, respecting the master undo ledger in `~/.openclaw/workspace/crm-undo/`.

Running the upgraded pristine pass yielded:
- **1,444 duplicate contact entries archived**.
- **3,149 attribute fields merged onto keeper records**.
- **0 remaining duplicate contact groups** in `v_contact`.

### 2. Close Friends & Family Tiering & Gap Closure
We established a canonical roster at `~/.sanctum/crm/close_friends_family.yaml` (with example in `sanctum-crm/config/close_friends_family.example.yaml`), tracking thirty-five close friends and household family members.

In a second optimization pass, we eliminated remaining gaps across the database:
- **Unicode Accent & Hyphen Folding**: French names differing only by diacritics (e.g. `Simon Fortin-Deschênes` vs `Simon Fortin-Deschenes`, `Frédéric Laurin` vs `Frederic Laurin`) were unified. Keeper records were scored to favor accented typography, upgrading 25 keeper names and archiving 121 duplicates while merging 446 fields.
- **Evidence-Based Birthday Discovery**: Cross-referencing AddressBook archives, macOS `Family` calendar recurring events, and iMessage history surfaced exact dates for contacts previously missing birthdays:
  - **Albert Nepveu**: `2010-07-30` (AddressBook & family messages)
  - **Jean-Pierre Nepveu**: `0000-09-26` (family messages)
  - **Catherine Nepveu**: `0000-11-24` (Family calendar & messages)
  - **Guillaume Thérien**: `0000-09-03` (messages)
  - **Vincent Dostie**: `0000-10-12` (messages)
  - **Patrick Byrne**: `0000-10-20` (messages)
  - **Martin Forget**: `1977-05-19` (turning 50; messages)
  - **Andréanne Charbonneau**: corrected from `0000-03-08` (historic Women's Day contact placeholder) to `0000-06-09` (actual annual family celebration).
- Total close circle contacts with verified birthdays in DuckDB expanded from 20 to **27**.

### 3. Gated Briefing Delivery
In `jocasta-preflight.py`:
1. `icalBuddy` now runs with `-ec "Birthdays,Birthdays1"`, cutting off the 4,000-contact AddressBook firehose entirely.
2. `get_close_birthdays()` queries `v_contact` in DuckDB, retrieving upcoming birthdays strictly for contacts tagged as `Family` or `Close Friend`.
3. Dates are resolved with accurate age calculation for full birth years and clean name formatting for year-zero entries.

### 4. Interactive Terminal CLI
Added `sanctum-crm birthdays [--days N] [--all] [--json]` to inspect upcoming birthdays across the close circle at a glance.
