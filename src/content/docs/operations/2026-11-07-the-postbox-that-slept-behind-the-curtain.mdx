Saturday morning on `manoir` brought three critical alerts across Force Flow: Jocasta's `crm-freshness` email channel (`8.1h > 6h` SLA), `proxyd`'s `config-drift` hot-reload check, and Chitti's seventh organ (`Endocrine GLAND_DOWN`, `panel stale 1179s`). ## 1. Jocasta's hidden postbox: two days, three silences

Interpreter-wide Full Disk Access stays off the table on `manoir` by Windu's doctrine. Jocasta therefore reads correspondence hourly through AppleEvents against a hidden background `Mail.app` (`open -gj -a Mail`). Putting `mail-sync.log` beside `crm-freshness.log` separated three distinct mechanisms:

- **Friday's real 39.6-hour server dormancy.** Inside Mail's sandboxed preferences, `PollTime` sat at `-1` ("Automatically"). Hidden with zero windows on a headless Mac Mini, `Mail.app` went idle and stopped polling Exchange and IMAP altogether. JXA still answered Jocasta in milliseconds from Mail's frozen local cache — returning the same forty overlap rows and exiting `0` while real mail piled up upstream. When `Mail.app` restarted Friday afternoon, Jocasta ingested **172 queued emails** in one run.
- **Saturday morning's genuine overnight quiet.** Friday's final email landed at `22:08` local time. Nobody wrote overnight. At `06:11` Saturday, message age crossed six hours (`8.1h`) and paged Force Flow even though all four upstream servers held nothing new.
- **The 18-day quiet-account cursor loop.** Whenever a quiet account such as iCloud caught up (`ceiling is None`) with zero new rows, `fetch_from_jxa` popped its slice cursor and re-walked ten 48-hour slices from scratch on the next run.

Jocasta now closes all three seams together in `sanctum-crm`. Before reading a single tray, `_sync_mail_servers` pins `fetch interval` to `5` minutes, invokes `check for new mail`, and drains `background activity count` to zero (relaunching Mail cleanly on `-1712` timeouts). Quiet caught-up accounts retain a rolling `now - 36h` cursor in `mail-jxa-cursor.json`. Finally, `_email_quiet_why` rescues overnight and weekend silence up to **18 hours** — but only when `mail-sync-status.json` proves an active server sync succeeded within `2.5h` with zero failed accounts and no newer candidate rows.

## 2. The deafened `proxyd` watcher and the starved Endocrine beat

Two sibling mysteries completed the morning audit. Neither was a fluke.

- **`proxyd` deafened after an `fseventsd` heal.** `proxyd` had started on October 26 and hot-reloaded `config.yaml` cleanly on October 27 (`loaded_at=1793131032`). On November 4 (`last_heal=1793848437`), Qui-Gon and `sanctum-chi fseventsd` reaped a bloated 3 GB `fseventsd` (`PID 48230 -> 67953`). On macOS, restarting `fseventsd` orphans existing `FSEventStream` Mach ports silently without emitting channel errors in `notify`. Both `config.yaml` and `budget.yaml` watchers sat permanently deaf until Friday evening's edit tripped `config-drift`. Worse, when `fseventsd` *was* alive, a single save fired eight un-debounced reloads in `97ms`. A new `watch.rs` supervisor now pairs `notify` directory events (`100ms` debounce + SHA-256 deduplication) with a **1-second `stat` + periodic SHA-256 fallback** on the same thread, automatically re-binding `RecommendedWatcher` whenever `stat-poll` catches a silent edit.
- **Endocrine `GLAND_DOWN` under `ProcessType=Background`.** Both `haus.sanctum.endocrine-gland` (`StartInterval=120`) and its sentinel ran under `ProcessType=Background` (`PRIO_DARWIN_BG` + `IOPOL_THROTTLE`). During Friday evening's GPU load (`1794011050..1794015464`), disk throttling starved cold imports of the full Typer and Rich CLI tree past the `25s` wall four beats in a row before `republish_checkpoint()` could even execute. Then `launchd` coalesced and deferred the `Background` timer past `600s` (`1179s` and `3468s`), aging both `panel.json` and `beat-seen` into `GLAND_DOWN`. Lifting the gland and sentinel LaunchAgents to `ProcessType=Standard`, routing `endocrine-tick.sh` straight into stdlib-only `gland_daemon --once --compact` (**60ms** vs **390ms**), and bounding worst-case HTTP timeouts (`11.5s < 20s` inner alarm) eliminated both failure modes.

| Subsystem & fault | Silent macOS mechanism | Permanent defense |
|---|---|---|
| Jocasta `Mail.app` harvest (`crm-freshness`) | Headless `PollTime = -1` stopped background Exchange/IMAP fetches; quiet nights breached 6h SLA | Active `check for new mail` + `background activity count` drain, pinned `now - 36h` cursor, and 7-gate `18h` sync-proof quiet rescue |
| `proxyd` hot-reload (`config.yaml` & `budget.yaml`) | `fseventsd` restart (`PID 48230 -> 67953`) orphaned `FSEventStream` Mach ports with zero errors | `watch::spawn_file_watcher`: `notify` (`100ms` debounce + SHA-256 dedupe) + `1s` `stat`/SHA-256 poll + automatic watcher re-bind |
| Chitti seventh organ (`haus.sanctum.endocrine-gland`) | `ProcessType=Background` deferred `StartInterval=120` past `600s` and throttled CLI imports past `25s` | `ProcessType=Standard`, stdlib-only `gland_daemon --once --compact` (`60ms`), and `11.5s` worst-case probe budget |

Every hidden curtain in the haus now answers to a direct mechanical check. Quiet nights sleep undisturbed, and real stalls surface on time.
