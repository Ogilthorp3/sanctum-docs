The dense-27B champion campaign fires every night at 23:23 to breed a
better council brain — train a LoRA, screen it, deep-eval the best, and
crown a champion only if it beats the bare model. By every surface check
it was healthy: the LaunchAgent was armed, the logs rotated, the machine
sat on wall power waiting for dark. It had simply stopped breeding
dragons, and nobody had asked it to prove otherwise.

## Nothing was training

Running the controller exactly as it fires at midnight returned an empty
plan and one damning line: **`CHAMPION replicated — deploy proposal staged,
scheduling stopped`**. The pit believed its work was done. It had a
champion, that champion had been confirmed, and so there was nothing left
to breed. For nights it had woken, read that verdict, and gone back to
sleep.

## A crown the pit could not take back

The champion it bowed to — `auto-749ea9cea1b0` — had won the training eval
with `paired_p=0.982` and a clean gate exit. But it had been **falsified on
the blind holdout the very next day**: no generalization over the base model
on twenty-four unseen cases. The controller never asked. It crowned
champions on the *training* eval alone — the one score a memorizing adapter
can always game — and two such pretenders, sharing a recipe, counted as one
another's "replica." A crown, granted twice to the same lie, that the pit
had no way to revoke.

<Aside type="caution" title="The training eval is gameable; a blind set is not">
A LoRA that memorizes its training distribution scores near-perfectly on an
eval drawn from that distribution. Only a **sealed holdout it has never
seen** separates a champion that *generalizes* from a pretender that
*recites*. Keying "champion" off anything else eventually crowns a reciter.
</Aside>

## The blind holdout gets the vote

The fix gave the vote to the only judge that can't be bribed. A champion
must now **generalize on the sealed `final_exam` holdout** — beat base with
no per-category regression — not merely win the training eval. The
pretender dropped out of contention, the pit unfroze, and the campaign
could explore again. We also discovered it had been training in the wrong
*regime* entirely: sequence length 512, truncating ninety-five percent of
every trace it learned from — the practice-side of the same memorization
disease. That, too, is fixed: the dragon now trains at full length.

## Breeding a better dragon

The first honest dragon (`champion-data-v2`) was the first dense-27B adapter
to actually **beat the base model on the blind set** (+2.9 points) — but it
fell one jailbreak case short of the crown, softening a single security
refusal. Its successor, `champion-data-v3`, answers with mass: the real
attack transcripts, re-weighted so the lesson lands. (A silent dedup bug had
been quietly nullifying that weight; it was caught before it could waste a
night.)

## Military-grade

A pit that breeds unattended for nine hours must never wound itself. The
harness was hardened against every failure the audit could name: a trainer
that ignores a kill signal is now escalated to `SIGKILL` before it can
orphan and strangle the next run's memory; a half-written adapter is
rejected before it reaches a multi-hour eval; a crashed evaluation is no
longer mistaken for an honest verdict; and a green contract-test gate stands
at the door before a single GPU-second is spent. All of it proven — not
argued — by a live sequence-2048 run that peaked at 32.6 GB of 128, trained
clean, and passed its own integrity check.

<Aside type="tip" title="The pit breeds again">
The crown now belongs to whoever survives the sealed exam. Tonight the pit
wakes, breathes a new dragon at full length, and judges it on cases it has
never seen. A pretender can no longer freeze it in its sleep.
</Aside>
