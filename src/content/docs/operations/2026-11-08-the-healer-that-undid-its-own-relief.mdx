Home Assistant Green had zero persistent notifications on its own board. Yet the operator's iPhone buzzed **78 times** between midnight and eight in the evening. Force Flow (`notifications.db`, sequence `58,844`) held the entire trail: every push arrived through the phone's companion-app notify service, and **73 of the 78 alerts (`93.6%`)** came from four loops that detected real trouble, ran a half-measure or undid their own repair, and paged again on the next timer tick.

## 1. Sixty seconds between relief and relapse

Forty-three of today's iPhone alerts (`217` across seven days) complained about swap on `manoir`. Two separate defects kept that fire burning.

First, `quigon-doctor.py` fought itself inside the same process invocation. At `15:08:17`, `17:11:02`, and `18:12:32`, `quigon-doctor.py --heal` invoked `memory-sentinel.py --relieve` at the top of its pass. Because the primary `:1337` [Cathedral](/operations/council-always-alive/) (`Qwen3.8-27B`) had been pushed past `75%` into swap, [The Pressure Valve](/operations/pressure-valve/) unloaded the `17.6 GiB` backup coder seat (`haus.sanctum.mlx-coder` on `:3301`) via `launchctl bootout` and recorded `last_shed` in `~/.sanctum/state/memory-sentinel.json`. Sixty seconds later in that **exact same run**, Qui-Gon walked its check table down to `devstral_3301`, saw TCP `:3301` closed, checked `under_pressure()` (which inspected only `chi.json` `ram_frac >= 0.92` and ignored both kernel memory pressure and `memory-sentinel.json`), and ran `enable+bootstrap haus.sanctum.mlx-coder`. Every hour, Qui-Gon parked seventeen gigabytes of weights and immediately hauled them back upstairs. Two minutes after reload, `devstral-prewarm-sentinel.sh` sent a nineteen-thousand-token sacrificial prompt into `:3301`, driving `kern.memorystatus_vm_pressure_level` straight back to `2`.

Second, `haus-pulse.py` still carried pre-dual-residency swap lines (`13.0 GiB` amber, `16.5 GiB` red) with no kernel pressure check at all. Steady dual residency on `manoir` sits at `14.5–18.5 GiB` swap at `pressure=1`, right across both tripwires. `haus-pulse` fired twenty-five gentle and loud transitions in a single Sunday.

Closing the loop took four coordinated edits:
- **`quigon-doctor.py` (`probe_coder_3301` & `under_pressure`)**: reads `~/.sanctum/state/memory-sentinel.json` so a backup seat intentionally parked by `memory-sentinel` reports `ok=True` (`parked by memory-sentinel`), and treats `kern.memorystatus_vm_pressure_level >= 2` as `pressured=True`.
- **`memory-sentinel.py` & `swap-sentinel.sh`**: raises `SHED_COOLDOWN` from `30m` to `2h` (`7200s`) and adds `--relieve --sustained` before `swap-sentinel`'s thirty-minute `(still)` repeat page; if shedding a fully-swapped backup drops kernel pressure back to `1`, `swap-sentinel` logs `self-healed-warn` instead of paging the iPhone.
- **`haus-pulse.py`**: aligns swap thresholds (`24 GiB` amber / `28 GiB` red + `pressure >= 2` / `4`) with `swap-sentinel` and calls `memory-sentinel.py --relieve` before raising a swap finding.

## 2. The airlock severed the CLI's mirror, not the daemon

Nineteen loud `error` pushes today (`47` across forty-eight hours, one every sixty minutes since Friday night) came from Qui-Gon's `yoda_identity` check:

```text
PLACEHOLDER/wrong identity: Account: +15555550101  Operator: +15555550100
```

Friday's [Four Hinges Behind the Airlock](/operations/2026-11-06-four-hinges-behind-the-airlock/) unmounted the host home directory inside `sanctum-vm`. Yoda's live `yoda-chat-proxy.service` unit kept working because its systemd drop-in (`10-operator.conf`) injected `YODA_ACCOUNT` and `YODA_OPERATOR`. However, `probe_yoda_identity()` ran `yoda-chat status` as an unprivileged CLI subprocess **outside** systemd. Without environment variables or the unmounted host `~/.sanctum/.instance.json`, `yoda_chat/config.py` fell back to its `+15555550xxx` scaffold defaults. `heal_yoda_identity()` then restarted `yoda-chat-proxy.service` without ever creating `~/.sanctum/.instance.json` inside `<VM>`, failed its post-heal probe eight seconds later, and paged the operator every hour with zero chronic backoff.

`vm-autostart.sh` and `heal_yoda_identity()` now project a secret-free, two-field `~/.sanctum/.instance.json` (`0600`, containing only `notifications.signal.{account, target}`) across the airlock before restarting `yoda-chat-proxy.service`. Running `heal_yoda_identity()` converged `probe_yoda_identity()` in two seconds (`identity bound to real account/operator`). In addition, `quigon-doctor.py` now caps identical non-converging escalations at two hourly pages before throttling to a twelve-hour digest (`ESCALATE_DIGEST_SEC=43200`), resetting immediately once a check turns green.
