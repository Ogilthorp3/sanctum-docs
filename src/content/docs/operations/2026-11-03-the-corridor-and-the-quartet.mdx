A static resting threshold does not know your autonomic nervous system, and it does not know Mounjaro. Cilghal learned that on Tuesday. Two days after [Every Dial at Rest](/operations/2026-11-01-every-dial-at-rest/) united Apple Health, Withings, confirmed neurotypes, and daily pharmacology across [The Holocron](/architecture/holocron-gui/), our morning dashboard still cried `low` recovery on a crisp, rested morning. Watch HRV read `29.66 ms`. Resting pulse sat at `49 bpm` after `7.69 hours` of sleep with `20.4%` deep slow-wave restorative stages. Any human coach would green-light training on the spot. A hardcoded `35 ms` floor could not.

## The corridor replaces the gate

Population HRV medians confuse inter-individual anatomy with intra-individual fatigue (`Plews et al., 2013 · PMID 23899752`; `Buchheit, 2014 · PMID 24791914`). One athlete thrives at `33 ms`. Another drags at `55 ms`. Meanwhile GLP-1/GIP agonists such as tirzepatide shift resting heart rate upward by `+2 to +4 bpm` through sinoatrial receptor pacing without any true sympathetic overreaching (`SURMOUNT-1 · PMID 35658024`).

We replaced the fixed cutoff with `coach/recovery.py`. On every `/twin`, `/coach`, and `/panel/` request, Cilghal computes the operator's personal 30-day rolling HRV median and interquartile `0.5 × IQR` smallest worthwhile change (`SWC`) corridor alongside a 30-day RHR baseline calibrated for active GLP-1 therapy (`+3 bpm` allowance) and deep-sleep architecture. Fresh installs with fewer than five watch nights fall back gracefully to population norms.

On `manoir`, `78` days of Apple Health history yielded a 30-day HRV median of `33.1 ms` (`n = 25`) and a 30-day RHR median of `52.0 bpm` (`n = 23`). Sitting at `90%` of personal HRV baseline with RHR `3 bpm` *below* baseline and `1.57 hours` of deep sleep immediately moved recovery from false-alarm `low` (`Lv 1/10`) to `good` (`Ready 84/100`, `Lv 8/10`), lifting overall Hero Level from `5` to `7`. Drop HRV below `78%` of corridor (`23.5 ms`) and spike RHR to `63 bpm`, and the amber `low` shield fires right on cue.

## Ninety days of horizon and four rings

Single-day snapshots also hid three months of quiet aerobic progress. Adding `coach/trends.py` and inline SVG sparklines across both `:8770/panel/` and `Santé` exposed what single numbers missed: over ninety days on `manoir`, `VO₂max` climbed from `30.7` to `33.6 ml/kg/min` (`+2.9`) while resting pulse dropped from `53` to `49 bpm` (`-4`).

Closing the remaining `11.4 ml/kg` gap to the `VO₂max 45` longevity horizon (`Mandsager et al., 2018 · PMID 30347096`) requires weekly rhythm rather than guesswork. The new **Weekly Longevity Quartet** (`GET /quartet`, `POST /quartet`, stored in `0600` `~/.sanctum/cilghal-coach/quartet.json` per household `profile_id`) pairs automatic Apple Watch workout classification with one-tap check-ins across four physiological pillars:

| Pillar | Weekly dose | Physiological target |
|---|---|---|
| `Zone 2 Aerobic Base` | `3 × 45 min` nasal pace | Mitochondrial biogenesis & capillary density |
| `Norwegian 4×4 VO₂max` | `1 × 4×4 min` hard intervals | Central stroke volume ceiling (`PMID 17414804`) |
| `Strength & Carries` | `2 × 40 min` heavy resistance | Type II fiber retention under GLP-1 therapy |
| `Mobility & Downshift` | `2 × 15 min` vagal work | Parasympathetic vagal tone & joint longevity |

Cilghal routes the single highest-yield next session dynamically. On a `low` readiness morning, she holds `4×4` intervals and routes to `Mobility & Downshift`. On a `peak` or `steady` day with `BDNF` Met-carrier or `COMT Met/Met` wiring, she prioritizes morning aerobic intervals to unlock maximal activity-dependent `BDNF` expression and prefrontal clarity.

## Bloodwork calibrated for creatine and Type O

Wearables see rhythm. Bloodwork sees cargo. Standard clinic portals routinely misread two harmless signatures in our stack: daily `5–10 g` creatine monohydrate converts at `~1–2%/day` into creatinine, nudging serum creatinine upward and depressing creatinine-based `eGFR` without touching true glomerular filtration (`ISSN · PMID 28615996`), while `O−` blood type paired with GLP-1 appetite reduction narrows ferritin reserves silently long before hemoglobin drops.

`coach/labs.py` (`GET /labs`, `POST /labs`, `0600` `~/.sanctum/cilghal-coach/labs.json`) adds a zero-cloud **Longevity Bloodwork Vault** tracking eight high-signal markers (`ApoB`, `Lp(a)`, `Cystatin C eGFR`, `Ferritin`, `HbA1c`, `hs-CRP`, `25-OH Vitamin D`, and `Homocysteine`):

- **Dual-unit Canadian SI & US normalization.** Whether a Québec lab prints `ApoB 0.72 g/L` and `Lp(a) 42 nmol/L` or a US panel reports `78 mg/dL`, Cilghal normalizes units deterministically and grades against longevity targets rather than disease-floor reference ranges.
- **Smart Paste & clean-room isolation.** Pasting raw portal lines into either UI parses markers locally via regex (`parse_lab_text`) with zero LLM calls and zero personal defaults in git. When creatine is active in `medications.json`, Cilghal explicitly flags `Cystatin C eGFR` as the true renal yardstick.

## Morning coffee, Factor V skies, and native Santé parity

Three proactive guardrails and full Electron parity completed the loop:

1. **Routine-anchored pharmacology & neuro nudges.** `coach/daily_feedback.py` and `coach/sleep_coach.py` weave dynamic chronotype (`morning`, `intermediate`, `evening`), `COMT`/`Orchid` acoustic wind-down cues, the `12:00` slow-`CYP1A2` caffeine cutoff, and the morning coffee creatine habit (`5 g` stirred warm + `4–5 g` second split on `9–10 g` brain-and-muscle days) into daily coaching.
2. **Pre-flight `Factor V Leiden` radar.** `GET /twin` scans a 14-day lookahead across local DuckDB `v_event` calendar rows for flight keywords (`flight`, `vol`, `airport`, `YUL`, `YYZ`, `CDG`, `LHR`). When a user carrying `F5` (`rs6025`) has an upcoming flight, both dashboards raise an amber hydration, aisle-walk, and `15–20 mmHg` graduated-compression checklist — calibrated honestly for `Type O` low-`vWF` attenuation.
