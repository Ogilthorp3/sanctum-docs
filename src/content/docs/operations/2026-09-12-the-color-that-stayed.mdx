The kitchen lights finally had the right tone. Not the stark 6500K clinical white that Tuya smart bulbs reboot into, and not the heavy candle-orange (hue 15, 100% saturation) reserved for late-night ambience in the corner and chalet. It was tungsten warm: **2000K incandescent filament**, mapped cleanly to `brightness: 255`, `hs_color: [29.0, 93.0]`, and `rgb_color: [255, 132, 18]` (`xy: [0.603, 0.377]`).

The request was simple: make that color permanent for the kitchen, and align the dining room, reading room (corner RGB), and corridor groups to the exact same Comfort setting in Home Assistant Green.

## The layers of amnesia

In Home Assistant, "permanent" is never one setting. Light bulbs are ephemeral hardware that drop offline, get power-cycled at the physical switch, and get repainted by morning routines and default profiles.

Forensic inspection of the HA Green appliance (`10.0.0.3:8123`) revealed why light tones kept drifting:

1. **`scenes.yaml` drift**: On 2026-08-18, `brightness` and `hs_color` had been stripped out of `light.kitchen` in `scene.bonjour_montreal`, leaving it bare (`state: true`). Furthermore, `light.dining_room` was not included in the morning scene at all.
2. **The partial Tuya script**: In `scripts.yaml`, `smartlife_comfort_mood` attempted to push raw Tuya DP25 scenes to only three of the six kitchen bulbs and one of the two dining bulbs, forcing them into `Mode Scene` while leaving the other bulbs unmanaged.
3. **The stranded package**: An automation package (`ha-green/packages/kitchen_comfort.yaml`) had been committed on the Mac Mini in August to catch power-reconnects, but was never deployed to HA Green's `/homeassistant/packages/` and never included in `configuration.yaml`.
4. **Missing default profile**: Without `/homeassistant/light_profiles.csv`, any unparameterized `light.turn_on` call defaulted to the bulb firmware's power-on memory.

## Multi-layer enforcement

To guarantee that Kitchen, Dining Room, Reading, and Corridor stay at the desired tungsten warm look across any condition, we landed a four-tier defense directly on HA Green and bridged to Sanctum:

### 1. Reconnect & Power-on Package
Deploys `packages/kitchen_comfort.yaml` loaded via `configuration.yaml`. Whenever any kitchen, dining room, reading (corner RGB), or corridor bulb transitions from `off`, `unavailable`, or `unknown` to `on`, HA Green catches it and applies `brightness: 255`, `hs_color: [29.0, 93.0]` after a 2-second settle delay.

### 2. Core Default Profiles
Updated `/homeassistant/light_profiles.csv` mapping `light.kitchen.default`, `light.dining_room.default`, `light.reading.default`, `light.corner_rgb.default`, `light.corridor.default`, and all thirty constituent bulb entities to `x=0.603, y=0.377, brightness=255`. Any call to `light.turn_on` without explicit color parameters defaults to tungsten comfort.

### 3. Morning Scene & Routine Alignment
- Updated `scene.bonjour_montreal` in `scenes.yaml` to explicitly turn on both `light.kitchen` and `light.dining_room` at `brightness: 255`, `hs_color: [29.0, 93.0]`.
- Rewrote `script.smartlife_comfort_mood` in `scripts.yaml` to enforce the full tungsten group mood across all four zones and their LocalTuya twins rather than a partial localtuya scene.
- Added `light.reading` group alias in `configuration.yaml`.

### 4. Bridge Readback Sentinel
Updated `haus.sanctum.ha-green-bridge` on the Mac Mini (`bin/ha-green-bridge.py`) to include `light.kitchen`, `light.reading`, and `READING_LIGHTS` in `WORK_LIGHTS`, verifying hardware readbacks continuously across all active bulbs.

## Disk & Repository Verification

All configs on HA Green were validated via `hag-core.sh check`, reloaded live via REST API, and synchronized into the `sanctum-config` repository under `ha-green/`.

Live state verification on HA Green confirms:
- `light.kitchen`: `state=on bri=255 hs=[29.0, 93.0] rgb=[255, 132, 18]`
- `light.dining_room`: `state=on bri=255 hs=[29.0, 93.0] rgb=[255, 132, 18]`
- `light.reading` / `light.corner_rgb`: mapped to Comfort profile in package, profiles, and bridge.
- `light.corridor`: mapped to Comfort profile in package, profiles, and bridge.
