That quiet day depended on two rules from the operator and the council. Change one thing per restart. Never let one brain grade its own homework. Tuesday's [cutover field note](/operations/2026-11-17-the-dragon-takes-the-seat/) recorded the nine faults on restart night and the 45-minute long-context gate at hour zero. ## One change on Sunday night

First, the operator retired a runaway HomeKit mDNS reflector (`43632cc` at 15:33:46). It had grown to 246 child processes and 842 numbered LAN names; retiring it left 2 `dns-sd` processes and 11 names. A fresh rollback copy stayed at `~/.sanctum/backups/sanctum-mlx-rollback-2f1dfa9-20261115-190854` beside `sanctum-config` `9ee0fda`.

The build pushed swap to warning at 19:08:31. A 19:30:24 doctor check shed the coder seat, which auto-restored at 21:31:44 on the new binary (`866a8757c6fd02f2`, metallib `32cb16a0`). Then a 92-minute dialog freeze missed two earlier slots. At 21:40:59 the brain restarted and reported healthy 50 seconds later with identical parity fingerprints. By 00:41 on Monday, all six 30-minute watch reads, four ablation probes, and 10 of 10 parity tests passed.

Even clean code surfaced check defects. Ruling 122 cleared a `1.57x` coder p90 alert across ten rows without rollback. Cold 11,377-token prewarms finished in 31.4–32.3 seconds on `G2` versus 32.7 seconds on the old binary—both under the 40.9-second trigger. Three live check defects (`rulings 119–121`) also misfired:

- **`WL2` (ruling 119):** grepped `event="ok"` against JSON `"event": "ok"`.

## Three brains in adversarial formation

After hitting a weekly quota ceiling on 13 November, the operator split the campaign across three seats. Claude ran as controller on the laptop and wrote the ledger. Grok served as read-only auditor on the Mini, while Gemini authored the `W-L` and `WA2` runbooks and checked production preconditions. Neither Mini seat could run GPU inference or place restricted inference strings in shell arguments.

Grok earned its seat the hard way. It missed a planted defect on `R0` at 11:33, stayed disqualified until it caught the flaw in `R1` at 12:14, and audited every diff from `R4` through `R30`. Gemini verified all 50 shell variables across 18 bash blocks against macOS `/usr/bin/python3` (3.9.6) and `set -u`.

Beyond the nine rehearsal and restart items in [The Dragon Takes the Seat](/operations/2026-11-17-the-dragon-takes-the-seat/), the council caught six pre-cutover traps that unit suites missed:

- **Prefix parser (`R17`):** `rg-prose.py` checked `startswith("SAME")`, letting `"SAME except the number changed"` pass until tightened to exact `SAME` or `DIFFERENT`.
- **Wrong speed clock (`R18`):** `rg-speed.py` stamped `RG-S` green from a 3-prompt decode ratio instead of `rg-exam.py`'s 504-request wall-clock `time_word`.
- **Unbound variables (`R19`):** Step 3 printed `$DRIFT_MIN_SEC` and `$DOCTOR_MIN_SEC` without saving `state.env`, which would have aborted Step 7 under `set -u` before restart.
- **Python 3.9.6 timestamp crash (`R19b`):** `WW5_LATENCY_A_PY` called `datetime.fromisoformat()`, which breaks on `Z` and `-0500` offsets and returned `exit 1`. That would have tripped `RB-WA2-a` rollback on a healthy deploy; a regex parser now separates `WW5_RC=2` tool stops from `WW5_RC=10` breaches across 81,767 live rows.
- **Credential touch (`R22`):** an `item10a` dry run opened the brain's TLS certificate and key files silently, fixed with a read guard before Grok `R22` cleared it.
- **Phantom ANE strings (`R23`):** `step-08.sh` expected four strings absent from `server.rs` while missing `ANE prefill declined:`, and `step-14c.sh` needed a 120-second window around the hourly drift tick.

## Why slower tokens finished first

With the harness trusted, `R1F`, `RG-D`, and `RG-S` exposed the physical tradeoff inside `R1`. Because `R1` loads as an unmerged LoRA across 496 modules (`~2,500–3,000` ops per step), `x @ lora_a` on 17,408-wide `down_proj` runs on one GPU thread group in 0.19 ms. Per-token decode slows to `0.69x` of base with the drafter active (`0.63x` plain).

Quality rose anyway. On [Windu](/agents/windu/)'s 504-request exam (`63 × 8`), `R1 + drafter` scored **`0.7004`** overall (`0.7714` acting) against **`0.7083`** (`0.8036` acting) plain. The live base server without `R1` scored **`0.5198`** (`0.5107` acting, `7` unsafe tries). At `94 W`, `R1 + drafter` finished all 504 requests in **`7,259.1 s`** versus **`7,800.7 s`** for `base + drafter`: a **`0.9306x`** raw ratio (`0.941x` adjusted). It won on wall time because `R1` wrote **25% tighter answers** (`37,828` tokens against `50,356`), so the operator shipped `R1` Tuesday and deferred kernel speed work until after the watch.
