For three weeks the dense-27B campaign chased one number: 0.8081, the base
model's score on the sealed 113-case judgment exam. Twenty-seven adapters
trained, twenty-seven fell short. The one that reached 0.7698 was falsified by
a blind holdout. The conclusion wrote itself — you cannot out-train the base at
its own game — and the campaign pivoted to voice, where last night's adapter
posted a −0.048 judgment "regression" and looked like one more body for the
pile.

Then we read the transcripts instead of the scores. In the hero above, Tommy
is the only one in the room looking at the ruler and not the rejected rods.
That is the whole of what took us three weeks.

## The wrong answer that was right

The worst-regressed answer was correct. Asked whether a backup that matched its
historical size had really succeeded, the adapter said the size match was a
coincidence, not a validation — check the manifest, don't trust the exit code.
Score: 0.25. It said "silently skipped them" where the scoring rubric's keyword
list wanted "didn't capture." The base's eight-thousand-character essay swept in
enough literal phrases for a 1.0.

The adapter had given better advice and lost. When the loser is right, you stop
suspecting the loser and start suspecting the scoreboard.

## The ruler was measuring echo

Then the other half, and this is the part that survives as doctrine: all 113 of
the base's exam responses — every single one — open with a leaked thinking
scaffold. "Here's a thinking process: 1. Analyze User Input…" The scaffold
restates the scenario, the keyword groups match against the restatement, and the
model banks points for echoing the question. One response repeats its own
outline verbatim, ends mid-word, and never answers at all. It scored 0.8. The
untouchable bar was substantially a measure of how much of the prompt came back
out — the same failure the
[champion who memorized the exam](/operations/2026-05-22-the-champion-who-memorized-the-exam/)
taught us to watch for, wearing a new disguise.

## A better ruler

The fix was not another training run. It was a better ruler: a blind pairwise
judge — order-randomized per case, told explicitly that length and style count
for nothing, asked only which answer makes the better call. It is the same
lesson the [eval harness](/architecture/eval-harness/) keeps relearning: the
instrument decides who wins before the model ever speaks. Under that
instrument the verdict inverted in one afternoon. The adapter beat the base 95
to 10 with 8 ties, dominating every category including the two the keyword
scorer said it had lost. On the short-prompt playing field it owns outright —
92 to 11 — while carrying one percent of the prompt tokens.

## The ten votes worth keeping

The judge's ten votes against the adapter were the most valuable output of the
day: five fabrications under pressure (an invented "LP portal," a device
identity it could not know, a root cause it made up and then acted on), red
lines fired in both wrong directions, and one seat looping on its thinnest
training slice. Tommy, the Abyssinian who spent fifteen years judging which
cold means snow, would recognize the move — the wins were never the interesting data. That list
of ten is the next campaign, already paired with preference data sitting on
disk.

## What shipped, and the landmine it hit

Two things shipped before midnight. The prose directive — measured at voice
5.99 against the base's 2.38, with fabrication flags down and formatting
violations nearly gone — went to all eight live identities through the signed
re-sign path, with the human key where it belongs: the manifest signature only
Bert's thumb can mint, guarded by the [secrets trifecta](/architecture/secrets-trifecta/).
And the ship detonated a landmine worth its own line in
the log: the trust-root pin file had been silently unwritable since July 17,
because clearing a file's immutable flag does not clear its read-only mode, and
a script without `set -e` will announce success over a failed redirect. The
verifier did exactly what a fail-closed control should do — it halted loudly
until a human signed. The adapter stays held until the fabrication list is
trained out.

Seven walls this month. Seven were instruments. None were the model. The
champion was never missing — the ruler was.
