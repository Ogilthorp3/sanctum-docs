## Teaching upgrades to refresh `launchd` Launch Constraints

In [The Sentinel That Patches the Fleet](/operations/2026-10-22-the-sentinel-that-patches-the-fleet/), we met `launchd`'s Launch Constraint (`LWCR`) cache on `tailscaled`. Calendar LaunchAgents fail more quietly. When Homebrew upgrades `/opt/homebrew/bin/bash` or `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3`, every loaded `StartCalendarInterval` plist still holds the old Mach-O CDHash (`needs LWCR update`). Nothing crashes at upgrade time. Instead, the job aborts days later when its scheduled window fires.

Both `~/.sanctum/windu/windu-threat-watch.py` (`_refresh_stale_lwcr_agents` across `manoir`, `mbp`, and `chalet`) and `~/.sanctum/bin/sanctum-sunday-upgrade.sh` (`lwcr_refresh_agents` in pure `plutil` + `launchctl`, zero Python) now close that gap automatically. After each upgrade, they scan calendar-only plists (`RunAtLoad=false`, `KeepAlive=false`) and evict stale CDHashes via `bootout` + `bootstrap` without triggering out-of-window runs.

## Giving `sanctum-power` and `wan-sentinel` real teeth

Wiring `sanctum-power.py` against the live EcoFlow Delta 3 Max Plus exposed two protocol traps. Synthetic fixtures never caught either one.

1. **Signed GET query parameters vs `Content-Type: application/json`.** EcoFlow's Americas IoT Open API (`https://api-a.ecoflow.com`) signs sorted query parameters (`sn=...&accessKey=...&nonce=...&timestamp=...`) with HMAC-SHA256. Sending `Content-Type: application/json` on `GET /iot-open/sign/device/quota/all?sn=...` tells the gateway to sign an empty JSON body instead, failing every call with `code=8521 signature is wrong`.
2. **Delta 3 protobuf v2 keys and the BMS float band.** On Delta 3 firmware, battery state lives under `cmsBattSoc`, `powGetAcIn`, `cmsChgDsgState`, and `cmsMaxChgSoc`. When USB-C ports draw 15–27W at `100%` charge on grid power, the BMS shuts off AC charging (`powGetAcIn = 0.0`, `cmsChgDsgState = 1`) and lets the pack drift 2–3% below `cmsMaxChgSoc` before topping back up. Treating `soc >= cmsMaxChgSoc - 3.0` as inside the BMS float band stops false `BATTERY` pages while leaving ~97% of a 2,048 Wh LFP pack to alert and halt cleanly at `15%`.

`haus.sanctum.wan-sentinel` now probes `cloudflare.com` (avoiding Firewalla's `one.one.one.one` DoH canary block) with `SANCTUM_WAN_AUTO_OFFLINE=1` armed. By contrast, `haus.sanctum.power` keeps `POWER_ARMED=false` until watched through a live mains pull: telemetry is green (`state: "GRID"`, `soc: 100.0`), and hardware halts wait for physical proof.

## Closing the loop across drills, rotation, Windu, and Cargo

- **Break-glass secret rotation (`~/Backups/rotate-secrets.sh`).** Updated preflight and rotation targets from retired localhost Docker to Home Assistant Green (`ssh green` `/homeassistant/secrets.yaml` + `ha core restart`), `~/.sanctum/secrets/{ha-token,firewalla-bridge-token,netcontrol-token}`, VM `openclaw.json` fallback, and `haus.sanctum.{firewalla,ha-green-bridge,ha-gateway,yoda-agent}` (`--dry-run` verified all `9/9` slots).
- **Chaos & monthly drills (`openclaw-skills`, `sanctum-runtime`, `~/.sanctum/drills`).** Pointed `chaos-forge/scripts/preflight.sh` at VM Docker (`6/6` preflight pass), retired `docker-oom.sh`, fixed `sanctum-drill-watch.sh` JSON quoting (`age_days: 1` against `reboot-2026-10-26.md`), aligned monthly drill labels to `haus.sanctum.*`, and verified `21/21` BATS assertions plus live `drill-jsonl-lock-flood.sh`.
- **Windu Argon sandbox & Rust advisories (`sanctum-rs`).** Adding `--sandbox` and `gemini-4-argon-low` to `ask_argon` cut advisory triage from a `180s` timeout to `~9s`; bumping `python@3.11` to `3.11.18` and widening Mach-O version probes to `15s` cleared runtime drift. In `sanctum-rs`, dropping `russh`'s unused `rsa` feature (Firewalla SSH uses `ED25519`) and updating `tokio` to `1.54.1` brought `cargo deny check` to zero advisories (`advisories ok, bans ok, licenses ok, sources ok`).

Grand walls rarely fail first. Sensors go blind, bottles shift under cached hashes, and drills keep grading last summer's machine. Close those seams, and the shield holds when the lights actually go out.
